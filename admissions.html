Primary Admissions & Vacancies

Simple, transparent enrollment steps for Nursery through Primary Seven.

1. Required Documents

Copy of child's birth certificate, immunization card (Nursery/P.1), previous school report (P.2–P.7), and two passport photos.

2. Friendly Placement Check

Pupils joining P.1–P.7 complete a relaxed literacy and numeracy readiness activity with our class teachers.

3. Flexible Fees & Bursaries

Termly installment options, sibling discounts, and merit bursaries are available for bright upper-primary learners.

Ready to Apply for a Child's Vacancy or Teaching Position?

Parent vacancy applications are hosted inside the Pupil Portal page, and teacher applications are inside the Staff Portal page.
